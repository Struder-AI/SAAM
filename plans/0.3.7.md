Gains: repair on Mac, no per-platform builds, no GPL component, one tolerance model. Risks: degenerate input (coplanar overlaps, near-touching sheets) and speed (native 15–17 s per brain). First step: a one-day spike on Thingi10K 44374/44375 and the duck 75443 measuring speed and parity with CGAL.
