# 0.4.0 developer guidance review

2026-10-20. Owner approved the proposed replacement below, including scoped
analysis in place of routine solving. The approved replacement is applied;
other permissions remain unchanged. [Release intent](0.4.0.md) owns
the request; [assessment](0.4.0-design.md#developer-guidance-retain-intent-reduce-incidental-obligations)
records the historical evidence and its limits.

## Replace the first two code-shape rules

Replace rules 1 and 2 under `Code shape` in DEVELOPER-CONTEXT.md and BUILDERS.md
with the following text:

> 1. State has an explicit owner. Local cursors, accumulators and private caches
> may mutate within that owner. Shared part state belongs to Bundle; private
> session, job and presentation state belongs to its controller. Ownership
> transfer and retained snapshots must be explicit.
> 2. Calls and callbacks obey the owning contract's declared plugs and effects.
> Named calls aid review; higher-order operations and selected callbacks are
> allowed when their interface, permitted effects and state ownership are clear.
> A callable value or indirect dispatch does not grant access to other modules.

This removes blanket bans on reassigned state/callable bindings and expression-
selected callees. It preserves actual state and interaction authority. The
observed historical triage found one callable among 163 reassigned bindings;
most were controllers, caches, cursors and accumulators. That evidence supports
removing incidental syntax constraints, but does not prove every old restriction
caused a defect.

Retain command/query separation, explicit ownership transfer, justified generic
routing, tolerance semantics, authorization, provenance and honest evidence.
Queries cannot influence command behavior. A command's outcome identifies its
results; separate read/delivery views preserve useful reports and artifacts.

## Ordinary development does not regenerate analysis

Replace the local standing instruction to solve after each work cycle with:

> Authored contracts are read directly. Run influence analysis only for a scoped
> enforcement investigation or an owner-requested audit; it is not a prerequisite
> for onboarding, editing, contract review or checkpointing. Report its coverage
> and unknowns separately from contract authority.

The later local full-solve deferral already prevents routine solving here. The
proposed replacement reconciles the contradictory standing instructions. It does
not delete the separate analysis tools or claim structural checks prove complete
causal conformance.

## Other guidance work within existing authority

Keep each current invariant with its owning contract and link to it from role
guidance. Preserve owner quotations and useful decision criteria. Correct stale
navigation, source/installed distinctions and current Export behavior. Do not
change dependency approval, no-new-tests/full-suite permissions, commit/push
authority, focus or runtime cleanup policy as part of this proposal.
