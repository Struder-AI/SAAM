## Bounded-memory execution

Owner addition to the migration goal, 2026-10-20: "Also include in 0.4.0 scope
that we need some way to deal with these memory issues that we are having. Can
we work in chunks?" The owner clarified that this concerns SAAM generation and
Scheduler, rather than agent conversation context. The owner's subsequent
clarification, 2026-10-21, broadens the requirement: "general chunking across
SAAM has not been completed" and "Saam should have general bounded-memory
execution everywhere that it matters." Generation/Scheduler fixes are part of
this requirement, not its full scope.

Identify the actual retained-data peak and implement bounded working memory at
the responsible operation boundary, using chunks or streaming where appropriate.
Keep global hard prerequisites, collision correctness, complete saved outputs,
cancellation and atomic publication. Existing minimal-effort travel and cooling
preferences remain contractual. Raising heap ceilings or silently truncating
work is not the requested solution. Verify a representative larger case and
report the memory bound and any remaining whole-input state.

Apply this across workload-sized Geometry/import/native operations, Regions,
construction and finalization, Scheduler, Bundle persistence and snapshots,
worker/IPC transfers, Studio loading/rendering, and Export/program/archive
production. Review extension inputs and outputs at their owning interfaces too.
Use streaming, chunks, lazy access, bounded caches, explicit ownership transfer
or temporary backing storage as appropriate. A compact full-result array is an
improvement, but does not establish bounded execution for an arbitrarily growing
workload. Complete results need not all reside in memory at once.

The primary agent authors resource obligations within the existing responsibility
contracts. Each significant path identifies its working unit, retained global
state, ownership and release point. Some algorithms or native kernels require
whole-object state; disclose and justify that requirement and its scaling rather
than claiming constant memory or weakening geometry/collision correctness.
Avoid unnecessary full-value copies across serialization, workers and consumers.
Inspection retains access to the complete value despite bounded display pages.

Acceptance covers representative growing workloads through the real entry paths,
including JavaScript/native memory and concurrent worker/transport copies where
applicable. Report peak working memory, remaining size-dependent state and
practical limits. Preserve exact data, immutable captures, global constraints,
travel/cooling preferences, cancellation, cleanup and atomic complete publication.
No silent truncation, arbitrary part-size rejection or heap increase substitutes
for this work. Current localized memory reductions do not complete this goal.

Integrated material-volume and realized machine-timing estimates are bonus
objectives removed from the required migration scope. A better heuristic can be
added later. Local material-per-distance, requested speed, stationary dose and
dwell remain instructions that consumers must preserve.

## SAAM responsibility contract

Approved by the owner, 2026-10-20, word for word:

> turn the person’s fabrication intent into geometry and machine-independent Paath, support their review in Studio, and translate their captured path and settings into a downloadable machine program when they choose Export.

Plugs define permitted crossings of a responsibility boundary. All other
cross-boundary interaction is forbidden by default. Command/query separation
remains in force. Queries must not behaviorally interfere with command flows:
they cannot change domain state, command decisions, ordering, cancellation or
published outcomes. Private presentation state and resource use confer no domain
authority.

User and agent are distinct entry plugs adapting shared commands and queries;
wrappers reuse the operation owners rather than duplicating their tools or
workflows. Keep two human review outputs: permanent Studio review, and a separate
development/debug channel exposing additional pipeline data. Both retain the
identity and completeness requirements of the viewed values.

The viewer aggregates wire contracts by projected endpoints, reveals their
constituents on click, and separates them in submaps where the endpoints differ.
Boundary plugs are green terminals. Owner clarification supersedes the earlier
incoming-left/outgoing-right arrangement for submaps: inherit the external peer's
position relative to the opened node on the immediate parent map. A peer at three
o'clock stays at three o'clock inside the submap, regardless of command/query or
input/output direction. One external responsibility has one green reference;
incoming and outgoing traffic alone do not create a split. If an interface truly
splits into distinct contracts, their references cluster at that position.
Those splits must actually partition the parent's contents and expose different
names, obligations or payloads; duplicated relationships are not a split.
Map-0 terminals remain directly movable. In submaps, plugs sit on an invisible
flattened oval with generous room for the default white contents. Size that oval
appropriately for those contents; keep its center and axes fixed during manual white-node
movement (after first display only the slider resizes it; see
[the 2026-10-26 oval rule](#functional-architecture-review)). White nodes may be
placed outside the circle. Drag internal nodes to
arrange; drag the background to pan.

## Paath and viewing data in a flow

> "Saampath", "paath" and even sometimes "path" will be considered equivalent from now on.

Paath is intended as a limited-degree NURBS curve carrying parameterized degree-1
anchors for effector pose, extrusion volume and velocity. Review additional
channels for necessity. The degree bound, interpolation semantics and full
contract remain to settle against existing consumers. Consider whether Trace
becomes ceremony, scheduling adds travel Paath, Studio can inspect Paath anywhere
in the chain, and Export translates it. Direct Paath firmware is a future
possibility, not a release requirement.

Owner refinement, 2026-10-20: "I don't think we need roll. Capable machine adapters
can derive it." Tool roll is not an intrinsic target Paath requirement. Rectangular
bead rate/aspect versus height/width anchors, point geometry, logical tool indexes
and scheduling metadata are under active review. Material per distance is the
owner's intuitive meaning of rate; compare flow per second too. Distinguish
optional integrated volume/time estimates from requested local material and
explicit stationary actions. [Paath decision proposal](0.4.0-paath.md) records
the three-agent research and concrete recommendations, pending review.

Owner clarifies that Scheduler may split an entity into new entities with their
own schedule indexes; point actions remain distinct from their arrival/departure
travel. Owner subsequently accepts nozzle-independent extension output with
neutral tool indexes. Scheduler receives a nozzle type (hole diameter/angle) for
each index and may use defaults. Machine assignment later maps the indexes to
concrete toolheads. The schedule's assumed envelope and actual-tool qualification
are explicit; see the [Paath proposal](0.4.0-paath.md).

Owner clarification, 2026-10-20: a producer of valid Paath supplies that guarantee
for the immutable value. Establish validity by construction or necessary checks
at authoring/import/publication boundaries and reuse it downstream. Finalization
applies substantive modulation, connections and finished-material publication;
it is not a compulsory admission pass for already-valid work. Transformations
establish validity of their changed results. Consumer-specific prerequisites,
source/schedule relations and machine compatibility remain their owners' work,
with guarantees reused for the same exact immutable inputs. An untrusted imported
assertion alone cannot establish validity.

> The contract for the viewers of any data has to be "this is an accurate and complete representation" of the same data any downstream consumer sees.

Provide live peeks at appropriate contract points in agent tool-call flows,
including CAD results and Paath. Assess useful points, a consolidated finite type
set, identity and completeness, cost, and views requiring significant new Studio
capability. Reconsider the CAD toolkit's name; no rename is settled.

## Semantic links — owner direction, 2026-10-21

> "I don't think value exists. There's just command and query."

> "Exactly, so control doesn't exist either. The link types are semantic in nature, and carry no non-semantic flavor. Make this change."

Command and query are the only architecture link kinds. This supersedes the
earlier value/control categories. Payload, transport, callbacks, observation and
lifecycle subject do not add kinds. Classify each interaction by the operation
requested: forward workflow, retention, cancellation and progress recording are
commands; read-only dependencies and status requests are queries. Preserve
existing payload meaning, immutable identities, authority and lifecycle guarantees.
The shared wire semantics own direction
and interpretation for both agents and the viewer.
