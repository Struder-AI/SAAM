# SAAM 0.4.0 intent

Intents from 2026-09-29 onward include [0.2.0](0.2.0.md) deferrals and supersede older 0.2.1 feature scheduling. [0.3.3](0.3.3.md) collects issues/upgrades while 0.3.2 is tested. Establish examples, priorities and acceptance before implementation; recording intent authorizes neither implementation nor hardware work.

## Scheduled and deferred intents

| Intent | Direction and open scope |
|---|---|
| General 3D solid Booleans and ownership | **Scheduled for 0.4.0 by the owner, 2026-10-03.** Establish robust union, intersection and difference, plus empty/containment/positive-overlap queries independent of print layers. Geometry owns one shared operation used by construction and effective spatial ownership; retain native representations where useful and do not promote display tessellation into manufacturing authority. Assess established algorithms/libraries, numerical guarantees, licensing, Windows/macOS deployment and measured time/peak memory before selecting a backend. Acceptance includes containment, touching/coplanar boundaries, thin overlaps, curved/mixed inputs, default remainders/reservations, repeated edits and large claims. Replace redundant ownership-validation slice probes; layer extraction/cache lifetime remains a separate concern. Assessment and scheduling are approved; backend choice and implementation design remain to settle. |
| Material overlap and shared ownership | Resolve overlapping claims before toolpath construction; first-defined explicit claimant is principal. Subordinate stacks adapt to its layer heights, retaining their own alignment/phase, infill, walls, material/process and outside orientation. Calculate interlock cadence jointly from all claimants, including their relative angles and pitches. Interlocking requires a minimum shared-claim depth, provisionally 0.1 mm, controlled by one named code-level setting for later adjustment (owner, 2026-10-15); this is independent of nozzle forgiveness. Preserve connected transitions; report incompatible overlap allocated solely to the principal. Acceptance varies claimant count, angle, pitch, phase, principal order and overlap depth around the threshold, checking contact, gaps, unintended duplicate deposition, transitions and exported results. |
| Scheduling and layer batching | Extend 0.2.0's dependency-aware ascending actual Z and preserved operation continuity with strategies justified by concrete multi-region/machine scenarios. Clearance and thermal planning are future work; do not simply restore a fixed number of heights per batch. |
| Carriage and tool collision avoidance | Model physical carriage/tool geometry and swept clearances before qualifying strategies such as finishing separated parts one at a time. Current visualization and bead clearance checks are insufficient. |
| Joined trimmed-face solids | Carry trimmed faces through closure, solid queries, display and deposition consumers; experimental surface records alone are insufficient. Previously assigned to 0.2.1 in [BR-059](../DEVLOG.md#br-059--retired-request). |
| General sleeves and family topology | Recognize and parameterize arbitrary bent mesh sleeves, beyond supported periodic patches, structured strips and contour-derived sleeves; address more general slice-family topology. |
| Constant-thickness offset stacks | General curved Slice stacks currently translate one reference in one direction, calibrated to a target mean normal gap; local gaps vary and set bead volume. Extend these to constant local normal thickness, resolving offset intersections. Specialized cladding already supports normal offsets. |
| Surface geodesic offsets (uncommitted) | Explore equal-distance fronts across imported mesh surfaces after establishing a concrete part/use case. General solid Booleans are now scheduled above; joined trimmed-face support remains a related explicit intent. |
| Move away from CGAL mesh repair (probable direction) | Owner direction 2026-10-03: likely drop CGAL in 0.4.0 because of recurring issues. Discuss replacement, retained repair capabilities and platform coverage before choosing an approach or assigning fixes. No replacement selected or removal authorized yet. |
| Support redesign | Revisit how supports are selected and planned beyond existing selected-contact, footprint and tree behavior; establish physical evidence. Previously scheduled after 0.2.1. |
| Design and load intent inference | Explore inferring intended material ownership or structural needs; keep this distinct from executing explicit authored claims. |
| Thermal and motion-aware ordering | Revisit heat balancing, lookahead and travel-time scoring, preserving existing dependency and cooling constraints. [D-026](../DECISIONS.md#d-026--closest-region-entry-first-defer-heat-considerations) records the deferral. |
| Capability discovery | Reconsider machine/skill discovery and extensible registration when multiple installations or independently installed capabilities create a need. [D-022](../DECISIONS.md#d-022--defer-automatic-capability-discovery). |
| Distance and deposited-segment query indexing | Owner, 2026-10-12: "Okay, these are definitely problems, but we will defer the fixes to 0.4.0. Ensure that intent gets written." Applies to accelerating solidDistance (currently scans every triangle per point) and curveSupportsPoint (currently searches deposited segments per point). Candidate methods discussed: a triangle bounding-volume hierarchy and a deposited-segment spatial index; method choice and measured acceptance remain to establish. The fixes are deferred from 0.3.7 to 0.4.0. |
| Vetted material library | Port the committed library concept after reviewing data and consumers against current machine/process contracts; do not restore withdrawn behavior. Committed and deferred by the owner, 2026-09-13: “mark material library as definitely we will port that over - but don't do it yet.” Starts only when the owner starts it. |

The rest of the saampath changes can wait until 0.4.0"). Studio draws only the SAAMpath, so its size is what every preview loads. |

## Possibilities without a settled requirement

- Refine vase offerings and add Trace extensions for useful examples, using [0.3.1's extension format](0.3.1.md).
- Keep modulation applications beyond visual/surface effects open. A general technical modifier system is not a settled requirement; graded infill density was not requested.
- Exact acceleration modelling was not a 0.2.0 gate; decide its value alongside motion timing before adopting it as an upgrade goal.

## Scope boundaries

Unfinished 0.2.0 work stays in [its acceptance plan](0.2.0.md), except integration explicitly taken into 0.3.1. Physical qualification continues across releases, with Bambu evidence and limits in [its output contract](../machines/bambu/SKILL.md). Software support is not print evidence.
