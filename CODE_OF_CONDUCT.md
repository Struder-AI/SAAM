# Code of Conduct

SAAM is a technical project about turning manufacturing intent into
machine-executed motion. Working on it should be straightforward and
low-drama. This document says what "straightforward" means in practice.

## Expected

- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree with a design, a pull request,
  or a review comment as specifically as you can.
- Assume good faith from other contributors, and extend the same
  correction-friendly attitude the project asks of its own evidence
  labels: being wrong once is normal; the point is to say so and fix it.
- Keep safety-relevant discussion (machine behavior, calibration, physical
  test results) accurate and unembellished. Do not round an experimental
  result up to a stronger claim than it earned.
- Respect maintainers' authority over project scope, roadmap, and
  compatibility policy as described in `PROJECT_CHARTER.md`, even when you
  disagree with a specific call.

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language or conduct,
  in any project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, or any
  community channel the project designates.
- Sharing another person's private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, reviews, or releases.
- Misrepresenting your own or another party's test results, credentials,
  or authority within the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, edit, remove content, or restrict participation in
response to a violation of this Code of Conduct. Serious or repeated
violations can result in a permanent ban from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report a concern through GitHub's private reporting on this repository, or
by contacting a maintainer directly if the concern involves the reporting
channel itself. Reports are handled as confidentially as the platform
allows.

This document may be revised as the project and its community grow.
