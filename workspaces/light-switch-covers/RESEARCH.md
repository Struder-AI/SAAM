## Evidence limits

Manufacturer dimensions describe particular products. Conversions retain published precision but do not add measurement accuracy. A module face, rocker size, box envelope, decorative frame and clear opening are separate quantities. Product drawings must establish corner radii, screw point coordinates, countersinks, rear pockets, clips and clearances before generation. Current and historic catalogs sometimes disagree; preserve SKU and revision rather than averaging dimensions. Search-index-only evidence for RACO 915C is flagged in the catalog.

Coverage includes major representative families rather than every commercial SKU. Additional country-specific families (including Korean, Israeli and other Nordic products, Chinese 118/120 formats, and legacy round/pushbutton plates) use measured/custom mode until primary drawings are added. Outdoor, wet-location, weatherproof, floor, fire-rated and structural device-mounting covers need distinct requirements and are not validated by this research.
